# Promotional email for housing societies

`index.html` is the email, `email.txt` is the plain-text version for mailing tools that ask for one.
The images load from this repository's GitHub Pages site, so **push to GitHub and turn on Pages first**;
then the email is also online at
https://chanchalsakardeqh.github.io/WATER-TANK-MANAGEMENT-SYSTEM-PRE-SALE-DEMO/email/

## Subject line (pick one)

1. Every drop counts: stop tank overflows in your society
2. Save water and pump electricity in your society
3. Your society’s tank levels, on every member’s phone
4. No more overflowing tanks or dry taps in your society

**Preview text** (shown next to the subject; already built into the email):
Live tank levels on every phone, overflow and leak alerts, and a motor that runs only as long as needed.

## Sending it from Gmail (a few societies at a time)

1. Open the email link above in Chrome on a computer.
2. Press **Ctrl+A**, then **Ctrl+C**.
3. In Gmail, click **Compose**, click in the message area and press **Ctrl+V**. The layout, buttons and images come across.
4. Add the subject line. Put your own address in **To** and the societies in **Bcc**, so they don't see each other's addresses.
5. **Send a test to yourself first**, and open it on your phone too.

## Sending to many societies

Use a mailing tool such as Brevo, Mailchimp or Zoho Campaigns. Choose "paste your own HTML" or "import HTML",
paste the contents of `index.html`, and add `email.txt` as the plain-text version. These tools add a proper
unsubscribe link and keep your Gmail account from being flagged for bulk sending.

## Good practice

- Send only to societies whose address you got from them or from a public contact, and honour every "Unsubscribe" reply.
- Address it to the right person when you can: change "Dear Committee Members" to "Dear Secretary" or a name.
- The "For example" box is an illustration with stated assumptions (1 HP pump, 15 extra minutes a day, 100 litres a minute).
  Keep the "Illustration only" line if you change the numbers.
- Enquiries from the WhatsApp button and QR code arrive with *(Ref: Email)*, so you can see how many came from this email.
